How Calculation Works

SGPA Formula

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) / Σ Credits

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Σ(Semester GPA × Semester Credits) / Σ Credits

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (SGPA - 0.75) × 10
What are credits?
Credits signify the academic weight or hours assigned to a specific subject. Core subjects usually carry more credits than labs or electives.
What are grade points?
Grade points are numerical values assigned to the letter grade you receive (e.g., O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8). It reflects your performance level in that subject.
The Logic
By multiplying your grade points by the subject's credits, the system ensures that your performance in major subjects has a proportionally larger impact on your final GPA.
